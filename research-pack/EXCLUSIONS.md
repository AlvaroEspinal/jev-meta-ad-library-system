# Exclusions and redistribution boundary

Only curated source, generic documentation, synthetic fixtures and local verification tooling are shipped. No node_modules, caches, .git history, .env/config files, browser profiles, cookies, credentials, registration files, client names/data, ads/media, prospect/company datasets, full books, full copyrighted transcripts/posts, API keys, private keys or model weights are included.

The Chrome manifest contains a public extension-identity key by design, not a signing key or secret. Synthetic negative tests may contain obviously artificial invalid passwords/URLs to exercise rejection. Marketing ideas are original summaries with attribution, not reproductions of books. External libraries are referenced by lockfile and obtained independently with their own licenses; dependency code is not bundled.

No public upload or cloud deployment has occurred as part of preparing this pack. Share this ZIP only through your chosen channel after independently confirming its checksum. Sharing grants no access to the sender's paid accounts, registered host, private vault or live ten-browser cockpit.
