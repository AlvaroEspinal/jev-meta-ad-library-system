# Glossary and folder map

- State: relevant decision-time evidence supplied to the model.
- Typed judgment: Choice, Score or Noul, not freeform execution.
- Evidence ID: stable pointer to an observed field/artifact; not proof of truth.
- Capture: stored source material; classification: a model/human label; acceptance: policy permits using a judgment; verification: independently checked outcome.
- Shadow: proposal logged without duplicate execution.
- Review: insufficient evidence, ambiguity or risk requires fallback/human attention.
- Awareness: intended prospect's knowledge; funnel: campaign/journey objective.
- Dominant angle: principal persuasion mechanism; hook: attention opening.
- Manifest: file/record metadata and hashes; checksum is integrity, not source authenticity.
- Cumulative history: deduplicated entities plus versioned observations across runs.

Folder map: docs/ original explanations and runbooks; schemas/ canonical vocabulary and portable record contract; fixtures/ synthetic examples only; scripts/ pure validator, replay, static demo and tests; MANIFEST.json hashes of distributable files; EXCLUSIONS.md omitted material and limitations.
