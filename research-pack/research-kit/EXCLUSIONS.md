# Explicit exclusions and limitations

Excluded: credentials/tokens/keychains, .env/config files, Chrome profiles/cookies/sessions, absolute machine paths, personal contact data, private client/account/CRM records, real competitor datasets/media, full books/transcripts/tweets/screenshots, vault/task databases, local model weights, installed native host, cloud/browser account setup and paid/live inference.

Included instead: original summaries, bibliography, canonical vocabulary/validator snapshots and wholly synthetic examples. No browser permission or source right is conferred by inclusion in this kit. The local ZIP is not uploaded/published. Hash manifests and pattern scans are evidence of checks, not a guarantee that all conceivable sensitive data or malicious behavior is absent. Offline tests are not production or model-quality certification.
